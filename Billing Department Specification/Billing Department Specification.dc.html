Billing Department Specification

Workflows, screen architecture, navigation, permissions and journeys for the Billing Executive, Billing Supervisor and Billing Admin. Source: BILLING_REQUIREMENTS v1.0 (MVP). Visual and layout rules: HMS Design Bible v1.0. No UI is designed in this document.

ROLE 1 · BILLING_EXECUTIVE
Billing Executive
Frontline counter. Creates invoices, collects payments and deposits, issues receipts, raises requests. Persona: Ritu Verma, Counter 1.
/billing
ROLE 2 · BILLING_SUPERVISOR
Billing Supervisor
Gatekeeper. Approves discounts, voids, refunds, write-offs; reconciles counters; watches receivables. Persona: Vikramaditya Rao.
/billing/supervisor
ROLE 3 · BILLING_ADMIN
Billing Admin
Department owner. Tariffs, packages, TPA & corporate contracts, policy, counters, rosters, reports, audit. Persona: Anita Desai.
/department/billing
In scope
Invoicing (OPD, ER, Day-care, IPD) · Multi-tender collection · Receipts · Deposits · Discounts · Voids · Refunds & credit notes · TPA cashless · Corporate credit · IPD running bill & discharge gate · Counter shifts · Reports · Audit
Out of scope
General ledger, journals, AP, depreciation, tax filing. Billing owns the patient revenue sub-ledger only and exports a daily settlement journal to ERP.
Bible standards applied
Every screen: Header → KPI (max 5) → Primary → Secondary → Action. Max 3 columns. Tables only for billing records, 56px rows, sticky header, search, filters, pagination. Forms ≤ 800px, sectioned. Money & IDs in tabular mono, ₹ with lakh commas.

1 · Business workflows

Thirteen workflows. W01–W05, W08, W11 come from requirements; W06, W07, W09, W10, W12, W13 are derived from stated responsibilities that had no workflow. Role tags: EXE Executive · SUP Supervisor · ADM Admin · SYS System / other dept.

W01 · OPD consultation & walk-in billing
Target ≤ 60s · E-02 → E-03 → O-01
Trigger
Check-in / doctor confirms consult; fee queues on UHID
Gate
Token moves to READY_FOR_CONSULTATION only on PAID
End state
Invoice PAID · INV + RCP issued · receipt delivered
  1. SYS Consultation fee queued as UNBILLED line, priced from Tariff Master by doctor + chamber + patient category.
  2. EXE Finds patient in queue or by UHID / mobile / barcode / national ID.
  3. EXE Reviews lines; may add services from Tariff Master (price read-only).
  4. EXE Applies policy scheme discount (auto, within ceiling) or raises discretionary request → W06.
  5. EXE Collects payment, single or split tender; records TID/auth code or UTR.
  6. SYS Issues INV-YYYYMM-XXXXX and RCP-YYYYMM-XXXXX; prints 80mm / A4 or sends WhatsApp / Email.
Exceptions: card/UPI failure → payment FAILED, invoice stays PENDING · partial payment → PARTIALLY_PAID, balance carried on ledger · discount pending → invoice held in DRAFT, patient waits or pays full and gets credit note on approval.
W02 · Pharmacy billing (OPD & OTC)
E-02 → E-03 → O-01
Trigger
Pharmacist confirms dispense (DISP-XXXX)
Gate
Medicine released only against receipt token
End state
Invoice PAID · prescription FULFILLED
  1. SYS Mode A: simple Cash/UPI settles at Pharmacy POS (outside Billing screens; appears in Billing reports).
  2. SYS Mode B: cashless, corporate or split → bill marked PENDING_AT_CASHIER and enters Billing queue.
  3. EXE Opens DISP bill, verifies batch lines (read-only), collects, closes invoice.
  4. SYS Prescription FULFILLED; patient claims medicine with receipt token.
W03 · Diagnostics (lab & radiology) — sample hard gate
E-02 → E-03 → O-01
Trigger
Test ordered in consultation → UNBILLED_DIAGNOSTIC
Gate
LIS locks sample collection until PAID / TPA-covered / IPD
End state
Gate released · phlebotomy unlocked
  1. EXE Reviews ordered tests; STAT orders sort to top of queue.
  2. EXE Reads out fasting / prep instructions (shown on line item).
  3. EXE Collects payment, or confirms coverage (corporate voucher / TPA approval).
  4. SYS Gate event to LIS; confirmation shown on receipt (“Sample collection unlocked”).
W04 · IPD admission deposit & running bill
O-02 · E-04
Trigger
Admission booked
Gate
Admission requires deposit receipt (or TPA / corporate cover)
End state
Live ledger until medical discharge
  1. EXE Collects deposit per bed class default (₹25,000 General, ₹50,000 ICU/Deluxe); issues DEP-YYYYMM-XXXXX.
  2. SYS Midnight accrual: bed, resident doctor, nursing. Real-time: ward pharmacy, OT slabs, diagnostics.
  3. SYS At 80% deposit utilisation → Interim Deposit Request alert to attendant and to E-04.
  4. EXE Collects top-up deposits; follows up on overdue requests.
Exceptions: ward transfer → tariff changes from transfer time · package admission → accruals inside package absorbed, exclusions billed separately.
W05 · IPD final bill & financial discharge clearance
E-04 → E-03 → O-01 → O-07
Trigger
Consultant marks MEDICALLY_DISCHARGED
Gate
Nursing & security release only with CLEARED pass
End state
Zero balance · QR Financial Discharge Pass
  1. EXE Pre-discharge checklist: pending lab results, pharmacy ward returns credited, OT lines posted.
  2. EXE Stops room-rent clock; compiles final itemised invoice.
  3. SYS Net = gross − discounts − deposits − TPA approved amount − corporate cover.
  4. EXE Collects patient share (co-pay, non-payables, balance) or refunds excess deposit → W08.
  5. EXE Issues clearance; SYS sets CLEARED and generates pass.
Exceptions: TPA final approval pending → status AWAITING_TPA, no pass · patient cannot pay → SUP approves credit discharge (undertaking) → W12.
W06 · Discount request & approval
O-04 → S-02
Trigger
Patient eligible for scheme or requests concession
Rule
Ceilings set by ADM in Governance (see §6 D1, D2)
End state
APPROVED applied to invoice · or REJECTED with reason
  1. EXE Picks scheme (Senior Citizen, Staff Family, Hardship…) at line or bill level; attaches evidence if the scheme requires it.
  2. SYS Within policy ceiling → auto-applied. Above → request created, invoice held in DRAFT.
  3. SUP Approves / rejects / modifies amount within own ceiling; above → escalates to ADM.
  4. SYS Real-time notification back to counter; approver stored in discount_approved_by.
W07 · Invoice cancellation / void
O-05 → S-02
  1. EXE DRAFT invoices can be discarded freely. Issued invoices: raises void request with reason code (wrong patient, duplicate, wrong service).
  2. SUP Reviews original invoice and payments; approves or rejects.
  3. SYS Invoice CANCELLED (never deleted). Any payment on it routes to refund (W08) or re-allocation to the corrected invoice.
  4. SYS Downstream reversal: lab gate re-locks if sample not collected; queue line returns to UNBILLED when appropriate.
W08 · Refunds & credit notes
O-03 → S-03 → E-06
Trigger
Cancelled consult / test, doctor unavailable, medication return, excess deposit
Rule
Refund to original tender; requester ≠ approver
End state
DISBURSED · CN-YYYYMM-XXXXX logged
  1. EXE Pulls invoice, selects lines and quantity, enters justification, attaches voucher → RFD-YYYYMM-XXXXX PENDING.
  2. SUP Verifies test unperformed (LIS status) / item returned to quarantine (pharmacy status). Approves or rejects with reason.
  3. EXE Cash: pays from drawer against signed voucher (counted in shift expected cash). Card/UPI: SYS triggers gateway reversal.
  4. SYS Credit note issued; audit captures requester, approver, reason, time, terminal IP.
W09 · Insurance / TPA cashless
E-04 · E-03 · S-05
  1. EXE Registers policy on patient account; SYS loads plan rules from contract (co-pay %, room-rent cap, exclusions).
  2. EXE Logs pre-authorisation request and approved amount; tracks enhancement requests as ledger grows.
  3. SYS Splits each line into payer share vs patient share (non-payables, room-rent proportionate deduction, co-pay).
  4. EXE At discharge: records final approval, collects patient share, compiles claim dossier.
  5. SUP Monitors claim receivable (submitted → settled / short-paid); short-payment → recover from patient or W12 write-off.
W10 · Corporate credit billing
E-03 · S-05 · A-05
  1. EXE Validates authorisation letter / voucher against active MOU (validity, signatory, credit limit, covered services).
  2. SYS Applies contracted rates; covered amount posted as CORPORATE_CREDIT tender; uncovered items collected from patient.
  3. SYS Blocks credit if limit or validity breached → SUP override or patient pays.
  4. SUP Generates periodic corporate statement; tracks receivable to settlement.
W11 · Counter shift open, close & reconciliation
E-07 · O-08 → S-04
Gate
No transaction without an OPEN shift on a counter
Formula
variance = physical − (float + cash in − cash refunds)
End state
Shift CLOSED · cash to vault · ERP journal
  1. EXE Selects counter, declares opening float. Every transaction tagged counter_id, cashier_user_id, shift_id.
  2. SUP Receives drawer threshold alerts mid-shift; can trigger cash pickup.
  3. EXE At close: denomination count (₹500…₹10), EDC batch total, UPI total, cheque list.
  4. SYS Computes variance per tender; non-zero requires explanation note.
  5. SUP Verifies cash bag, signs off or opens investigation. Shift → CLOSED or CLOSED_WITH_VARIANCE.
W12 · Outstanding follow-up & write-off
S-05 → S-02 → A-01
  1. SYS Ages balances into 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ by payer type (patient, corporate, TPA).
  2. SUP Sends reminders, records promises-to-pay, flags disputes.
  3. SUP Approves bad-debt / charity write-off within delegation limit; above → ADM.
  4. SYS Write-off posts as adjustment with reason; invoice remains, balance zeroed.
W13 · Master data governance (tariffs, packages, contracts, policy)
A-02 … A-09
  1. ADM Creates or edits a record as a DRAFT version with effective-from date.
  2. ADM Previews impact (e.g. affected services, open IPD ledgers, active packages).
  3. ADM Publishes; SYS keeps prior version. Open invoices keep the price at time of charge.
  4. SYS Every change audited (before / after, user, time). Counters and rosters follow the same publish model.

1.1 · Status models

One status vocabulary used across every screen. Colour tokens: Paid #15803D · Pending #B45309 · Void #DC2626 · Corporate/TPA #4338CA · Neutral #6B7280.

Invoice
DRAFT → PENDING → PARTIALLY_PAID → PAID · CANCELLED · REFUNDED
Queue line
UNBILLED · UNBILLED_DIAGNOSTIC · PENDING_AT_CASHIER → BILLED
Payment
SUCCESS · FAILED · REFUNDED
Deposit
ACTIVE → EXHAUSTED · REFUNDED
Request (discount / void / write-off)
PENDING → ESCALATED → APPROVED · REJECTED · WITHDRAWN
Refund
PENDING → APPROVED → DISBURSED · REJECTED
IPD clearance
RUNNING → MEDICALLY_DISCHARGED → FINAL_BILLING → AWAITING_TPA → CLEARED
Counter shift
OPEN → CLOSING_SUBMITTED → CLOSED · CLOSED_WITH_VARIANCE
TPA claim
PREAUTH_REQUESTED → PREAUTH_APPROVED → ENHANCEMENT → FINAL_APPROVED → SUBMITTED → SETTLED · SHORT_PAID
Master record
DRAFT → SCHEDULED → ACTIVE → RETIRED

Cross-department entry
Doctor / Nurse / Lab see read-only billing status chips and the discharge pass; they never enter billing routes.
