Billing Administration Architecture

How the Billing Admin layer governs revenue above the Counter and Supervisor workspaces: role hierarchy, workspace switching, information architecture, screen-by-screen specification, workflows, permissions, alerts and controls. Visual language and layout rules follow the HMS Design Bible and the existing Supervisor screens.

What already exists
Counter: Dashboard, Invoice Queue, IPD Running Bills, Receipts & Invoices, My Requests, Counter Shift. Supervisor: Dashboard, Approvals, Refunds, Counter Closing, Credit & Outstanding, Audit Stream, Shift Reports. Admin v2: Overview, Tariff Master, Packages, Insurance & TPA, Corporate, Counters, Staff & Shifts, Reports, Governance, Audit Log.
What this adds
Workspace switching, a Billing Manager tier, and nine screens: Revenue Integrity, Reconciliation, Patient Ledger, Deposits, Collections, Treasury, Period Close, Tax, Master Data. Four existing screens are extended: Packages, Insurance & TPA, Corporate, Governance (split into Approval Matrix and Policy Rules).
Principles
One owner per number: departments own service definitions; Billing owns price, policy and money. Every financial state change is reversible only through a new record, never an edit. Every approval has a requester ≠ approver. Every screen answers “what needs action now” before “what happened”.

2 · Workspace switching architecture

Fixes the current dead end: a supervisor can enter Counter Mode but has no way back. Implemented in the Supervisor and Counter prototypes.

Model
A workspace is a role + scope pair: Supervisor · Shift 1, Counter 2, Billing Admin. A user has one home workspace and may switch into any workspace their role permits. The session keeps the signed-in identity; only the workspace changes.
Switcher
Top bar, right side, on every screen: Workspace · Billing Supervisor ▾. The menu lists the home workspace first, then permitted counters with cashier and shift status. Cashiers do not see the switcher. In counter mode the control turns amber and reads Viewing as Supervisor · Counter 2 ▾, with “← Supervisor Dashboard” as the first item.
Context banner
Sticky amber strip under the top bar while in counter mode: “Viewing as Billing Supervisor · Counter 2. You are assisting on Amit Sharma’s shift…” with a solid Return to Supervisor Workspace button. Sidebar profile shows the supervisor, not the cashier.

Edge cases
Supervisor leaves counter mode with a payment drawer open → prompt to finish or discard; draft is kept on the patient. Cashier closes shift while supervisor is inside → banner switches to “Shift closed — read-only”. Two supervisors in one counter → second sees “Also viewing: Leena George”. Who sees what
Billing Manager sees A-01 (Manager view), A-03, A-04, A-07, A-08, A-09, A-10, A-11, A-13 (day close), A-20. Insurance Coordinator sees A-07 and A-10. Collections Executive sees A-09 and A-10. Treasury Officer sees A-12 and A-13 (cash steps). Internal Auditor sees everything read-only. Configuration (A-15 to A-19) is Admin only.
Shared screens
Patient Ledger (A-10) is one screen reached from everywhere: counter workspace, supervisor approvals, collections tasks, insurance cases. Same layout, actions filtered by role. Investigation drawer (O-10) and Approval drawer (O-11) are shared overlays used by Revenue Integrity, Approvals, Tariff, Collections and Period Close.
Navigation rules
Sidebar 260px, one active item, badges show items needing action (not totals). Top bar: breadcrumb, period chip (“October 2026 · open”), workspace switcher. Global search (/ or Ctrl K) finds patient, invoice, receipt, claim, MOU, tariff code, and opens the right screen. Every KPI card and alert deep-links to a filtered list. Tabs inside a screen, never nested sidebars.

5 · Workflows

Role tags: EXE Executive · SUP Supervisor · MGR Manager · ADM Admin · CFO · INS Insurance · COL Collections · TRE Treasury · SYS system or department.

W-A1 · Tariff revisionA-05 · A-15
  1. DEPT Owning department submits a price change with justification and effective date. Change is a draft and invisible at counters.
  2. SYS Sync record created (Pending review); impact computed (volume, packages, insurer schedules).
  3. ADM Approves, rejects, or requests revision with a note. Above +15% or Admin limit → routed to CFO.
  4. SYS Approved: new price version from effective date; open bills keep old price. Rejected: returned to department. Every step audited.
W-A2 · Pre-auth to settlementA-07 · A-10
  1. INS Creates pre-auth from admission or OT booking; uploads documents; submits on insurer portal and records it.
  2. INS Answers queries within 24h; records approval, partial approval or rejection; raises enhancement as ledger grows.
  3. SYS Counter sees approved amount and patient share; alerts at 90% utilisation.
  4. INS At discharge: final approval recorded, claim dossier checklist completed, claim marked submitted.
  5. MGR Settlement received: reconcile remittance (amount, TDS, deductions); short-payment routed to patient share or write-off by matrix; denial goes to appeal queue.
W-A3 · Corporate credit visitA-08 · counter
  1. EXE Scans employee ID; system validates against employee list, dependant, MOU status, limit and covered services.
  2. SYS Pass: corporate tariff applied, covered amount posted as credit. Fail: reason shown; patient pays or supervisor overrides within band.
  3. SYS Month end: statement generated per MOU; receivable aged.
  4. COL Follows up past SLA; disputes logged against invoices.
W-A4 · Leakage to recoveryA-03
  1. SYS Detects mismatch: department event without charge, discharge without final bill, package overrun, duplicate bill, discount or refund above pattern.
  2. SYS Scores risk; items over threshold alert Manager.
  3. MGR Assigns owner. Owner confirms with department: convert to charge, mark false positive, or open investigation.
  4. ADM Investigation closed with finding; repeated user patterns go to Internal Audit.
W-A5 · Deposit transfer or refundA-11
  1. EXE Raises transfer (to family member or new admission) or refund of unused balance with consent form.
  2. MGR Approves within band; above → Admin. Refund to original tender only unless Manager overrides with reason.
  3. SYS Ledger entries on both patients; receipt and credit note issued.
W-A6 · Cash: drawer to bankA-12 · A-14
  1. SUP Pickup at 80% drawer limit or shift close; sealed bag, count recorded by cashier and supervisor.
  2. TRE Receives bag, counts, logs into vault register (dual custody).
  3. TRE Prepares bank deposit; cash-in-transit handed to courier with seal numbers.
  4. TRE Next day: verifies bank credit; mismatch opens investigation automatically.
W-A7 · Period closeA-13
  1. SUP All shifts closed and signed off.
  2. MGR Department reconciliation signed; vault verified; unbilled items resolved or carried with reason. Day closed.
  3. ADM Month: all days closed, GST return generated, ERP journal posted, credit notes final. Month closed.
  4. CFO Year close after audit adjustments. Reopen of any closed period needs CFO approval, reason and a time limit.
W-A8 · Supervisor assists at a counterWorkspace switch
  1. SUP Picks Counter 2 from the workspace switcher; WORKSPACE_ENTER logged.
  2. SUP Verifies a payment, approves the cashier’s discount within band, corrects a draft. Actions post as assisted_by.
  3. SUP Returns with “Return to Supervisor Workspace”; WORKSPACE_EXIT logged with duration and actions.

8 · Audit, controls & edge cases

Controls that apply to every screen, then the cases most likely to break a naive design.

ImmutabilityFinancial records are never edited. Corrections are new entries (credit note, adjustment, reversal) linked to the original.
Segregation of dutiesRequester ≠ approver; cashier ≠ vault receiver; tariff proposer (department) ≠ publisher (Billing).
Dual custodyEvery cash handover recorded by two named people with seal numbers.
Period locksClosed day, month and year reject entries; reopen is time-boxed and CFO-approved.
Price at time of chargeOpen bills keep historical prices across tariff versions.
Workspace auditEnter, exit, assisted actions and denied attempts are logged with terminal and IP.
Merged UHIDsLedger shows both histories with a merge marker; balances combine; open approvals move to the surviving UHID.
Insurer approves after dischargePatient already paid share → excess refunded by credit note to original tender.
Package patient moves to ICUPackage suspended from transfer time; itemised billing until stepped down; Manager decides conversion.
Corporate MOU expires mid-admissionAdmissions before expiry stay covered to discharge; new visits blocked.
Tariff changes during stayCharges before the effective date at old price, after at new; shown as two lines.
Cashier unwell mid-shiftAdmin force-closes; supervisor counts; variance attributed to the shift, not the person, until investigated.
Refund to closed cardManager approves alternate tender with reason; bank transfer only.
Reopened monthEvery entry posted while reopened carries the reopen reference; auto-locks at time limit.
