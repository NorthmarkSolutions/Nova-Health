## 1. Requirements & Department Scope

### 1.1 Department Purpose
The Billing Department serves as the central financial clearinghouse and revenue settlement engine for North Hospital. It consolidates all clinical, diagnostic, pharmaceutical, procedural, and accommodation charges generated across the hospital, transforms them into formal legal invoices, collects payments across multiple tender modes, manages deposits and refunds, and provides immutable fiscal auditability.

### 1.2 Core Scope (MVP)
The Billing Department is strictly responsible for:
1. **Invoice Creation**: Auto-generating and manual consolidation of patient bills across encounters (OPD visits, emergency trauma episodes, day-care procedures, IPD inpatient admissions).
2. **Payment Collection**: Multi-tender payment capture (Cash, Credit/Debit Card, UPI / QR, Netbanking, Cheque, NEFT/RTGS, Advance Deposit adjustment, Corporate Sponsorship, TPA Cashless Insurance).
3. **Receipt Generation**: Printing formal 80mm thermal receipts and full A4 GST-compliant detailed itemized receipts, with digital delivery via Email and WhatsApp.
4. **Refunds & Credit Notes**: Structured initiation, review, approval, and disbursal of refunds and issuance of financial credit notes.
5. **Discount Governance**: Discretionary line-item and bill-level discount management with role-based ceiling enforcement and mandatory justification.
6. **Insurance / TPA Billing**: Managing cashless pre-authorization tracking, co-payment / deductible collection, claims dossier compilation, and claim receivable tracking.
7. **Corporate Billing**: Managing credit agreements, employee credit sponsorship bills, approval vouchers, and corporate receivables.
8. **IPD Running Bills & Discharge Clearance**: Real-time accumulation of ward bed charges, doctor rounds, nursing tariffs, surgical procedure slabs, lab diagnostics, and pharmacy ward issues into a live patient ledger, culminating in the mandatory **Financial Discharge Clearance Gate**.
9. **Counter Management & Day-End Closing**: Daily cash float assignment, physical counter collections reconciliation against system registers, discrepancy flagging, supervisor sign-off, and cash handover.
10. **Reports & Revenue Analytics**: Daily collections summary, payment mode breakdown, revenue by department, outstanding aging analysis, refund logs, and cashier productivity metrics.

### 1.3 Department Boundaries & Non-Goals
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Accounting System Separation of Concerns:**  
> The Billing Department **does NOT manage general accounting journals**, double-entry general ledgers (GL), depreciation schedules, vendor accounts payable (AP), or tax filing books.  
> Billing is responsible for the **Operational Patient Revenue Sub-Ledger**. Summarized settlement totals and counter closings export to external ERP/accounting suites (SAP, Tally, Oracle Financials) via daily reconciliation journals.

```
                      ┌────────────────────────┐
                      │ Reception / Reg Token  │
                      └───────────┬────────────┘
                                  │ Consultation Fee
                                  ▼
┌─────────────────┐       ┌───────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
│ OPD Doctor      │──────►│    BILLING    │◄──────│ Diagnostic Lab  │
│ Consultation    │  Rx   │  DEPARTMENT   │ Orders│ (Sample Gate)   │
└─────────────────┘       └───────┬───────┘       └─────────────────┘
                                  │
          ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
          │ Requisitions          │ Accrued Charges       │ Dispense Fees
          ▼                       ▼                       ▼
┌─────────────────┐       ┌───────────────┐       ┌─────────────────┐
│ Pharmacy Stores │       │ IPD Inpatient │       │ Operation Theat.│
│ (OPD/IPD/Store) │       │ (Running Bill)│       │ (Surgery Slabs) │
└─────────────────┘       └───────────────┘       └─────────────────┘
```

1. **Doctor Consultation (OPD)**:
   - When an appointment or walk-in is checked in, consultation charges are queued immediately.
   - Doctor prescribing generates medication orders, not financial lines; billing occurs upon dispensing.
2. **Pharmacy Department (OPD & IPD)**:
   - Pharmacy dispensing fires settlement engine events. Patients may pay directly at the Pharmacy POS Counter or route charges to the Central Billing Reception.
   - For IPD patients, medication issue commits automatically to the inpatient running bill ledger.
3. **Diagnostic Laboratory**:
   - Laboratory test orders generate queued billing charges.
   - **Sample Collection Hard Gate**: Phlebotomists cannot collect samples or print barcoded vacutainer labels until the bill is marked `PAID` (or approved under Cashless TPA / IPD admission).
4. **IPD Ward & Admissions**:
   - Admission requires an initial deposit receipt.
   - Daily midnight cron jobs accrue room/bed tariff and nursing fees.
   - Ward requisitions (consumables, medication) update the running ledger in real-time.
   - **Discharge Gate**: Patients cannot receive final nursing discharge clearance without a zero-balance Billing Clearance Slip.
5. **Operation Theatre & Surgery**:
   - OT booking and surgical pack utilization log surgeon fees, anesthesia fees, and theatre hourly slab tariffs directly into the patient's billing encounter.

### Role 1: Billing Executive / Cashier (`CASHIER` / `BILLING_EXECUTIVE`)
The operational frontline user located at hospital billing counters (OPD Main Lobby, Emergency Counter, IPD Discharge Desk, Diagnostics Cash Desk).

#### Responsibilities
- **Patient Lookup**: Search patient records via UHID, mobile phone, barcode scan, or national ID.
- **Queue Processing**: View queued charges originating from OPD consultations, lab test requisitions, radiology, and pharmacy.
- **Invoice Generation**: Consolidate unbilled clinical charges into a formal tax invoice.
- **Multi-Tender Payment Collection**: Accept Cash, Credit/Debit Cards (recording terminal TID & auth code), UPI (dynamic QR scan / transaction UTR), Netbanking, and Cheque.
- **Deposit Acceptance**: Collect advance admission deposits, surgery deposits, and patient wallet top-ups with immediate deposit receipts.
- **Split Settlement**: Process split tenders (e.g. ₹2,000 Cash + ₹3,500 UPI + ₹1,000 Wallet Deposit).
- **Receipt Dispatch**: Print 80mm thermal receipts or full A4 GST itemized receipts; trigger automated Email and WhatsApp PDF delivery.
- **Refund Requests**: Initiate basic refund requests with clinical notes, attaching payment receipt vouchers for supervisor review.
- **Credit Notes**: Generate credit note requisitions for returned items or billing adjustments.

#### Explicit Restrictions ("Cannot")
- ❌ **Cannot Approve Discounts**: Cannot apply discretionary line or invoice discounts above 0% without supervisor sign-off.
- ❌ **Cannot Approve Refunds**: Cannot disburse funds from cash drawers without authorized supervisor approval.
- ❌ **Cannot Cancel Invoices**: Cannot void or delete issued invoices.
- ❌ **Cannot Modify Tariffs**: Cannot alter service prices, consultation rates, or lab investigation fees.
- ❌ **Cannot Modify Billing Rules**: Cannot toggle tax codes, TPA caps, or credit ceilings.

**Demo Persona:** Ritu Verma (`EMP-BILL-01`), Senior Cashier · Counter 1  
**Default Route:** `/billing` or `/billing/cashier`

---

### Role 2: Billing Supervisor (`BILLING_SUPERVISOR`)
The operational controller and gatekeeper responsible for shift integrity, compliance, discount governance, and financial exception handling.

#### Responsibilities
- **Discount Authorization**: Review and approve/reject cashier discount requests against hospital ceiling policies (e.g., Senior Citizen, Staff Family, Hardship Waiver).
- **Refund Approval**: Investigate and approve clinical refund requests; verify returned items or unperformed investigations before releasing funds.
- **Bill Cancellation & Voiding**: Review cancellation requests for erroneously punched bills; enforce cancellation reason auditing.
- **Outstanding & Credit Bill Monitoring**: Monitor pending OPD credit balances, corporate unbilled vouchers, and overdue accounts.
- **Bad Debt Write-Offs**: Review and approve bad debt / charity write-offs up to authorized supervisor delegation limits.
- **Counter Monitoring**: Track live cashier collections, queue turnaround times, and drawer cash thresholds.
- **Shift Reconciliation & Counter Closing**: Verify physical cash counts against system registers at shift handovers, investigate cash variances, approve counter closing slips, and safe-deposit cash.

#### Explicit Restrictions ("Cannot")
- ❌ **Cannot Modify Master Pricing**: Cannot alter baseline service charges, surgery packages, or room tariffs.
- ❌ **Cannot Alter Core System Billing Rules**: Cannot modify GST rates, department revenue shares, or insurance master contract tables.

**Demo Persona:** Vikramaditya Rao (`EMP-BILL-SUP`), Billing Shift Supervisor  
**Default Route:** `/billing/supervisor`

---

### Role 3: Billing Admin (`BILLING_ADMIN` / `DEPARTMENT_ADMIN` with department `billing`)
The department owner and strategic administrator responsible for enterprise tariff design, contractual schedules, staff rosters, and compliance audits.

#### Responsibilities
- **Tariff Master Architecture**: Maintain hospital master price lists for all billable services (consultations, bed types, pathology, radiology, nursing, OT equipment, cafeteria, ambulance).
- **Package Pricing Management**: Construct composite bundled packages (e.g., Normal Delivery ₹45,000, Total Knee Replacement ₹1,85,000, Executive Health Checkup ₹5,500) with rule-based inclusions and exclusions.
- **Corporate & Institutional Pricing Master**: Define corporate discount schedules, credit limits, authorized signatory rosters, and validity dates for corporate accounts (e.g., ONGC, IOCL, Infosys).
- **Insurance Plans & TPA Rate Contracts**: Configure insurer tariff schedules (CGHS, Star Health, HDFC Ergo, Max Bupa) with specific co-pay percentages, deduction caps, and room rent ceilings.
- **Discount & Waiver Policy Engine**: Set departmental discount rules, role-based approval thresholds, and mandatory documentary evidence rules.
- **Billing Counter Management**: Configure physical billing counters, designate service points (Emergency, OPD, IPD, Diagnostics), and assign counter hardware (receipt printers, POS terminals).
- **Staff Duty & Counter Rostering**: Schedule billing executives and supervisors across Morning, Evening, and Night shifts.
- **Comprehensive Financial Reports**: Access enterprise revenue reports, collections by tender, GST tax summaries, aging reports, bad debt analytics, and staff productivity audits.
- **Audit Logs & Governance**: Monitor immutable log streams of every financial adjustment, discount override, cancellation, and refund.

#### Explicit Restrictions ("Cannot")
- ❌ **Cannot Execute Direct Clinical Care**: Does not prescribe medication or document clinical consultations.
- ❌ **Cannot Mutate General Ledger Directly**: Emits structured settlement journal feeds to finance rather than manual accounting journal edits.

**Demo Persona:** Anita Desai (`EMP-BILL-ADM`), Head of Billing & Revenue Cycle  
**Default Route:** `/department/billing` or `/billing/admin`

## 3. Business Workflows

### Workflow 1 — Outpatient (OPD) Consultation & Walk-in Billing
```
[Patient Arrival] ──► [Doctor Consultation] ──► [Charge Queued]
                                                      │
[Completed] ◄── [Receipt (Thermal/A4)] ◄── [Cashier Collects] ◄── [Bill Review]
```
1. **Charge Generation**: Patient arrives for doctor consultation. Doctor confirms consultation in HMS; consultation fee auto-queues under patient's UHID.
2. **Billing Queue**: Billing Executive opens `/billing/cashier` and finds patient in the active queue or searches UHID.
3. **Review & Cross-Sell**: Cashier reviews consultation line item, confirms doctor specialization and chamber tariff. If patient requests routine preventive tests (e.g. CBC, Lipid Profile), cashier adds service items from Tariff Master.
4. **Discounts (if applicable)**: If discount requested (e.g. 10% Senior Citizen), cashier enters discount code; system flags for Supervisor Approval if exceeding cashier threshold.
5. **Payment Collection**: Cashier selects tender mode (Cash, UPI, Card). System confirms receipt of funds.
6. **Receipt Dispatch**: System generates unique Invoice Number (`INV-YYYYMM-XXXXX`) and Receipt Number (`RCP-YYYYMM-XXXXX`). Cashier prints 80mm thermal receipt or sends PDF link via WhatsApp/SMS.
7. **Status**: Consultation marked `PAID`. Doctor queue updates patient token to `READY_FOR_CONSULTATION`.

---

### Workflow 2 — Pharmacy Billing (OPD & OTC)
```
[Pharmacist Dispenses]
         │
         ├───► Pay at Pharmacy Counter (Integrated POS) ───► [Invoice Settled]
         │
         └───► Pay at Central Billing Reception ─────────────► [Receipt Validates Release]
```
1. **Dispense Event**: OPD Pharmacist verifies e-Prescription, allocates FEFO medicine batches, and confirms dispense.
2. **Settlement Mode Determination**:
   - **Mode A (Fast-Track Pharmacy POS)**: Patient pays directly at the pharmacy counter via Cash/UPI. Pharmacist acts as cashier, enters tender, and prints receipt.
   - **Mode B (Central Reception Settlement)**: For cashless, corporate, or split-settlement patients, pharmacy marks bill `PENDING_AT_CASHIER`.
3. **Reception Payment**: Central Cashier fetches queued pharmacy bill `DISP-XXXX`, collects payment, closes invoice, and marks prescription `FULFILLED`. Patient presents receipt token at counter to claim bagged medication.

---

### Workflow 3 — Diagnostic Laboratory & Radiology Billing
```
[Test Ordered in Clinic]
         │
         ▼
[Bill Generated in Lab Queue] ──► [Payment Collected (Cash/Card/TPA)]
                                                  │
                                                  ▼
                       [SAMPLE COLLECTION HARD GATE CLEARED]
                                                  │
                                                  ▼
                                     [Phlebotomist Collects Sample]
```
1. **Clinical Test Requisition**: Doctor checks lab tests (e.g., Liver Function Test, HbA1c, Chest X-Ray) in clinical consultation.
2. **Queue Placement**: Lab orders appear in Billing Queue flagged `UNBILLED_DIAGNOSTIC`.
3. **Payment Collection**: Patient arrives at Diagnostic Billing Counter. Cashier reviews ordered tests, confirms fast/prep instructions, and collects payment.
4. **Sample Collection Hard Gate Release**:
   - Until invoice status is `PAID` (or authenticated as Covered by Corporate/TPA), the Laboratory Information System (LIS) **locks sample collection**.
   - Upon payment confirmation, LIS unlocks the token; phlebotomist can scan patient wristband and print barcoded vacutainer tube labels.

---

### Workflow 4 — Inpatient (IPD) Running Bill & Discharge Financial Clearance Gate
```
[Patient Admitted] ──► [Advance Deposit Paid]
         │
         ├──► Day 1..N Accruals (Bed Tariff + Doctor Rounds + Nursing + OT + Lab + Meds)
         │
         ▼
[Doctor Signs Medical Discharge]
         │
         ▼
[Billing Consolidates Final Invoice]
         │
         ▼
[Deposit Adjusted + Co-Pay/Balance Paid]
         │
         ▼
[FINANCIAL DISCHARGE CLEARANCE GATE GRANTED]
         │
         ▼
[Nursing & Security Release Patient]
```
1. **Admission Deposit**: Upon IPD admission booking, cashier collects initial deposit (e.g. ₹25,000 for General Ward, ₹50,000 for ICU/Deluxe Suite) and issues an Advance Deposit Receipt.
2. **Continuous Tariff Accrual**:
   - **Midnight Room Cron**: System automatically logs bed charge, resident doctor fee, and nursing care fee every 24 hours.
   - **Ward Requisitions**: Medicines issued by IPD Pharmacist attach immediately to running bill.
   - **Diagnostics & OT**: OT surgery slabs, anesthesia consumables, and bedside portable diagnostics attach automatically.
3. **Interim Monitoring**: Billing Executive monitors running ledger vs initial deposit. If running bill exceeds 80% of deposit, an **Interim Deposit Request** alert is sent to patient's ward attendant.
4. **Medical Discharge Trigger**: Treating Consultant marks patient `MEDICALLY_DISCHARGED`.
5. **Final Bill Consolidation**:
   - Central Billing Executive conducts final audit: verifies all pending lab reports, stops room rent clock, audits pharmacy returns from ward, and compiles Final Itemized Invoice.
   - Total Gross Amount calculated. Advance deposits deducted. TPA insurance approval voucher adjusted.
6. **Settlement**: Patient settles net out-of-pocket balance.
7. **Clearance Gate Release**:
   - System sets IPD Financial Clearance to `CLEARED` and generates a QR-coded **Financial Discharge Pass**.
   - Ward nurse cannot complete final discharge packaging and Hospital Security cannot authorize gate exit without the `CLEARED` pass.

---

### Workflow 5 — Refunds & Credit Notes
```
[Refund Requested by Cashier] ──► [Documentary Evidence & Notes]
                                                │
                                                ▼
                                   [Supervisor Review & Audit]
                                                │
                 ┌──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┐
                 ▼                                                             ▼
           [REJECTED]                                                      [APPROVED]
      (Reason Recorded)                                                        │
                                                                               ▼
                                                                [Disbursed via Original Tender]
                                                                               │
                                                                               ▼
                                                                  [Credit Note Issued & Logged]
```
1. **Refund Trigger**: Patient requests refund due to cancelled consultation, doctor unavailability, cancelled diagnostic test, or authorized medication return.
2. **Initiation**: Billing Executive pulls original invoice, selects line item(s), specifies refund quantity, enters mandatory justification, and attaches supervisor notification.
3. **Supervisor Gate**:
   - Billing Supervisor reviews request in `/billing/supervisor?tab=approvals`.
   - Checks that test was unperformed or medication returned to store quarantine.
   - Approves refund.
4. **Disbursal**:
   - Cash refunds are paid directly from cashier drawer with signed refund voucher.
   - Card/UPI payments trigger gateway API reversal or credit note creation.
5. **Audit Trail**: Generates Credit Note (`CN-YYYYMM-XXXXX`). Writable audit log records requester, approver, reason, timestamp, and terminal IP.

---

### Workflow 6 — Daily Counter Shift Opening & Closing Reconciliation
```
[Shift Start: Opening Cash Float Counted]
                  │
                  ▼
[Day Operations: Cash, Card, UPI, Cheques Collected]
                  │
                  ▼
[Shift End: Cashier Submits Physical Tender Tally]
                  │
                  ▼
[System Compares System Register vs Physical Handover]
                  │
                  ├─── Exact Match ───► [Supervisor Signs Off ──► Shift Closed]
                  │
                  └─── Variance Found ─► [Discrepancy Note Flagged ──► Investigation Logged]
```
1. **Counter Opening**: Cashier logs in, selects physical counter (e.g. Counter 2 - OPD Lobby), and enters opening cash drawer float (e.g. ₹5,000 in change).
2. **Operational Shift**: All transactions are tagged with `counter_id`, `cashier_user_id`, and `shift_id`.
3. **Closing Submission**: At end of shift, cashier initiates Counter Closing:
   - Cashier enters physical denomination breakdown (counts of ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10 notes).
   - Cashier enters total Credit Card EDC machine settlement batch totals and UPI transaction sums.
4. **Reconciliation Audit**:
   - System computes variance: `Physical Cash - Expected System Cash`.
   - If variance is 0, closing is green. If variance exists (over/short), cashier must input explanation note.
5. **Supervisor Sign-Off**: Billing Supervisor reviews counter closing, verifies physical cash bag handover, and signs off. Shift status marks `CLOSED` and cash transfers to hospital main vault safe.

## 5. Screens & Layout Specifications

### 5.1 Role-Specific Screen Inventory (MVP)

#### A. Billing Executive / Cashier Screens
1. **Billing Dashboard (`/billing`)**: Real-time counter status, queue count, shift collection meter, quick action buttons (`New Bill`, `Accept Deposit`, `Close Counter`).
2. **Invoice Queue (`/billing/queue`)**: Filterable stream of pending charges (OPD Consultations, Lab Investigations, Radiology Scans, Pharmacy OTC).
3. **Patient Billing Workspace (`/billing/workspace/:uhid`)**: 360° patient account view displaying unbilled lines, previous invoices, deposit balances, and active insurance coverage.
4. **Payment Collection Modal / Slide-over**: Multi-tender split calculator, cash change assistant, card terminal transaction ID, UPI dynamic QR code trigger.
5. **Receipts & Invoices History (`/billing/receipts`)**: Searchable repository of generated invoices, reprints, and delivery log tracker.
6. **Refund Requisition Drawer (`/billing/refunds/new`)**: Line-item selector, payment voucher reference, and mandatory clinical justification.

#### B. Billing Supervisor Screens
1. **Supervisor Dashboard (`/billing/supervisor`)**: Operational throughput overview, pending approval counters, active cashier list, cash threshold alerts.
2. **Approvals Queue (`/billing/supervisor/approvals`)**: Dual-pane review for discount authorizations, bill voiding, and credit balance write-offs.
3. **Pending Payments & Credit Review (`/billing/supervisor/credit-bills`)**: Unsettled corporate accounts, pending TPA claims, and overdue OPD patient accounts.
4. **Counter Closing & Cash Settlement (`/billing/supervisor/counter-closing`)**: Real-time counter reconciliation, cash-in-drawer tally, variance investigation, and bank safe handover sign-off.
5. **Refund Management (`/billing/supervisor/refunds`)**: End-to-end refund authorization, original payment verification, and credit note issuance.
6. **Audit Stream & Flagged Events (`/billing/supervisor/audit`)**: Real-time compliance feed highlighting price overrides, cancelled tokens, and high-value cash settlements.

#### C. Billing Admin Screens
1. **Admin Master Dashboard (`/department/billing`)**: Executive financial overview, revenue trajectories, billing department efficiency, and debt aging.
2. **Tariff Master Manager (`/department/billing/tariffs`)**: Global service directory, department charges, doctor consultation fees, and procedure slabs.
3. **Package Management (`/department/billing/packages`)**: Surgical and maternity bundle creator with inclusion/exclusion rules.
4. **Insurance & TPA Contracts (`/department/billing/insurance`)**: Third-Party Administrator master, co-pay rules, claim forms, and policy exclusions.
5. **Corporate Pricing Master (`/department/billing/corporate`)**: Corporate credit agreements, discounted rate schedules, and credit validity limits.
6. **Staff & Shift Scheduling (`/department/billing/staff`)**: Roster allocation of cashiers and supervisors across hospital counter stations.
7. **Billing Counters Directory (`/department/billing/counters`)**: Physical counter hardware registry, designated functions, and status.
8. **Reports & Revenue Analytics (`/department/billing/reports`)**: Tax summaries (GST), revenue by department, collection by tender, aging analysis, and cashier productivity.
9. **Governance & Permissions (`/department/billing/settings`)**: Maximum cashier discount ceilings, refund approval rules, and audit retention policies.

## 7. Antigravity Technical Implementation Architecture

### 7.1 Backend Architecture (`backend/apps/billing/`)

#### Relational Data Models
1. **`Invoice`**:
   - `id`, `invoice_number` (Unique, e.g. `INV-2610-00142`), `patient` (FK), `encounter_type` (`OPD`, `IPD`, `EMERGENCY`, `DAYCARE`), `status` (`DRAFT`, `PENDING`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `PAID`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`), `total_gross`, `discount_amount`, `discount_reason`, `discount_approved_by` (FK), `tax_amount`, `net_amount`, `paid_amount`, `balance_amount`, `counter` (FK), `cashier` (FK), `created_at`.
2. **`InvoiceItem`**:
   - `id`, `invoice` (FK), `department` (e.g. `OPD`, `LAB`, `PHARMACY`, `IPD`, `OT`), `service_code`, `service_name`, `unit_price`, `quantity`, `discount`, `tax_rate`, `net_price`, `source_reference_id` (FK to consultation, lab order, or pharmacy dispense order).
3. **`Payment`**:
   - `id`, `payment_number` (`RCP-2610-00142`), `invoice` (FK), `patient` (FK), `amount_paid`, `tender_mode` (`CASH`, `CARD`, `UPI`, `NETBANKING`, `CHEQUE`, `DEPOSIT_DEDUCTION`, `INSURANCE_TPA`, `CORPORATE_CREDIT`), `transaction_reference`, `auth_code`, `payment_status` (`SUCCESS`, `FAILED`, `REFUNDED`), `counter` (FK), `cashier` (FK), `created_at`.
4. **`PatientDeposit`**:
   - `id`, `deposit_number` (`DEP-2610-00042`), `patient` (FK), `ipd_admission` (FK nullable), `deposit_amount`, `utilized_amount`, `available_balance`, `tender_mode`, `status` (`ACTIVE`, `EXHAUSTED`, `REFUNDED`), `cashier` (FK), `created_at`.
5. **`RefundRequest`**:
   - `id`, `refund_number` (`RFD-2610-00012`), `invoice` (FK), `payment` (FK), `requested_amount`, `reason`, `status` (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `DISBURSED`), `initiated_by` (FK), `approved_by` (FK), `disbursed_at`, `notes`.
6. **`BillingCounter` & `CounterShift`**:
   - Counter code (`CNT-01`), station location (`OPD Main Lobby`), current active cashier, opening float, opening time, closing physical cash, system expected cash, variance, supervisor sign-off.
7. **`TariffMaster` & `ServicePackage`**:
   - Standard tariff items, service codes, department mappings, base prices, emergency markups, corporate contracted rates.

#### Service Layer Architecture
- **`BillingCoreService`**: Invoice generation, item consolidation, discount application, multi-tender settlement.
- **`IPDRunningLedgerService`**: Real-time accumulation of charges during admission, deposit balance reconciliation, discharge clearance verification gate.
- **`CounterClosingService`**: Shift opening, register aggregation, physical denomination reconciliation, cash handover.
- **`RefundWorkflowService`**: Refund validation, supervisor approval hook, credit note generation.
- **`TariffPricingService`**: Dynamic pricing calculations based on patient category (General, Corporate, TPA, Staff).

## 8. Verification & Acceptance Criteria

1. **OPD Consultation Settlement**: Patient checked in for doctor visit is billed, paid via Cash/UPI, receipt generated, and marked `PAID` with consultation queue updated in < 60 seconds.
2. **Laboratory Hard Gate**: Patient with unbilled lab order cannot be sample-collected until cashier records payment; collection unlocks immediately upon payment confirmation.
3. **Pharmacy Dispense Linking**: Medicine dispensed at Pharmacy either settles at Counter POS or routes to Central Reception with exact batch ledger consistency.
4. **IPD Running Ledger & Clearance**: Inpatient deposits are tracked against running bed, lab, and medication charges; medical discharge requires zero-balance Financial Discharge Pass before exit.
5. **Supervisor Control**: Cashier cannot apply discounts > 5% or disburse refunds without real-time supervisor authorization.
6. **Shift Closing Integrity**: Cashier shift handover requires physical note breakdown; variance is audited and supervisor-signed.
7. **Performance**: All table search, UHID lookups, and queue filters execute within ≤ 200ms.
